Strategy in Business Legacy:

Definition:
Strategy refers to the long-term planning and decision-making process that guides business growth.

Approach:
Business Legacy focuses on structured and forward-thinking strategies rather than short-term decision-making.

Key Elements:
- Setting clear long-term goals
- Identifying growth opportunities
- Analyzing strengths and weaknesses
- Planning for future challenges

Importance:
A strong strategy ensures that all business activities are aligned and contribute to sustainable growth.

Outcome:
Businesses with clear strategies grow more efficiently, reduce risks, and achieve long-term success.
