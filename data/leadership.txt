Leadership in Business Legacy:

Definition:
Leadership is a critical component in building a successful and scalable business.

Approach:
Business Legacy believes that leadership should not be limited to the founder. Instead, leadership should be distributed across the organization.

Key Concepts:
- Developing strong decision-making skills
- Building accountability within teams
- Encouraging ownership and responsibility
- Creating leaders at multiple levels

Benefits:
Strong leadership ensures that businesses can operate efficiently, handle challenges, and grow without depending on a single individual.

Outcome:
Effective leadership helps organizations become more stable, adaptable, and capable of long-term growth.
